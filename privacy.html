Privacy

Version 4.2 · 23 October 2026 (nutriDL 3.16: details what Open Food Facts can see when you scan a code)

In short

  • nutriDL has no accounts and no server of its own: everything you log is stored only on your device, in your browser's storage.
  • We use no advertising cookies, no third-party analytics, and we don't sell data. Your data is not used to train any artificial intelligence.
  • You can download a copy of your data, or delete all of it, whenever you want.

What data the app stores

What you enter: profile name, sex, age, weight, height, activity level, goal, weight and body measurement entries, food diary, foods and meals you save, and your workouts. Some of this is health data; that is why it stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

If you activate nutriDL PRO (trial or code), your device stores that it is active and until when. Your food preferences (diet type, allergies, foods you don't like, what you have at home), your daily menu, your shopping list, your achievements and your conversation with the Coach (the last 40 messages) are also stored on your device. The Coach runs on your device: your questions are not sent to any server.

The app also stores on your device an error log (the last 30) and usage measurements: counters (for example, how many times you open each tab) and the days you use the app, to know whether you come back the next day or the next week. None of it leaves your device: only you see it under “Error report”, and you decide whether to copy and send it.

Before an error is stored, email addresses, phone numbers, long numbers and any text in quotation marks are removed, so nothing you typed is kept. The app also notes whether it closed unexpectedly last time (because of a crash or low memory), with only the date and the version, so it can be fixed.

Automatic backups: once a day the app saves a copy of your data on your device (the last 3 are kept) so you can undo an accidental deletion from “Automatic backups”. These never leave your device either, and they are removed by “Delete all data”. The app also asks the browser not to delete its data when space runs low.

Food database: values for generic foods (raw and cooked) come from USDA FoodData Central (public domain), branded products from Open Food Facts (ODbL licence), and fast food from the official nutrition information each chain publishes in Spain. They are included in the app: looking them up sends nothing.

When something leaves your device

  • Barcode scanner: when you scan or type a code, only that number is sent to Open Food Facts to get the product's nutrition information. Your name, weight, diary and any other app data are never sent. As with any website visit, Open Food Facts may see your connection's IP address and browser type; that is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Camera: used only while the scanner is open, to read the code. Images are not stored or sent.
  • Fridge photo: like the meal photo, it is only shown on your screen while you tick what you have. It is not stored or sent.
  • Meal photo: the photo is only shown on your screen while you mark what is on the plate. It is not stored or sent.
  • Voice logging: dictation is done by your browser with its own service (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari), which may process the audio on its servers under its own terms. We explain this and ask for your agreement before using the microphone for the first time. nutriDL only receives the text and does not store the audio. If you prefer, you can type instead of speaking.
  • Backups and PDFs: these are files created on your device; you decide whether to share them.
  • Scanner limits: at most 8 products a minute are looked up on Open Food Facts, and never the same one twice in the same session, so as not to overuse their free service.
  • Hosting: the site is served from GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical connection data (such as the IP address) to provide the service and for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Your rights

Because the data is on your device, you control it directly:

  • Access and portability: profile menu → “Save backup” downloads all your data as a file.
  • Erasure: “Delete all data” (in the profile menu or in the page footer) removes everything from nutriDL on this device. It is also deleted if you clear the site's data in your browser.
  • Rectification: you can edit or delete any entry from within the app.

Controller and contact

Controller for nutriDL: its developer (Trbs-ALDL). For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights, write to aannggeelltrabajos@gmail.com. As we do not store your data on any server, the fastest way to exercise them is from the app itself (see “Your rights”).

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Minors

nutriDL is intended for adults (18 or over). The formulas it uses are not validated for minors.

Changes

If accounts, cloud sync, payments (nutriDL PRO) or artificial-intelligence features running on a server are added in future, this policy will be updated beforehand and your explicit consent will be requested to process health data. You will still be able to use nutriDL without an account.
