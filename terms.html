What nutriDL is

nutriDL is a free tool to estimate your calories and BMI, log what you eat and record your workouts. It is free to use and needs no sign-up.

Health notice

nutriDL is educational and for guidance. Its calculations are based on general-population formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor, WHO classification) and do not replace advice from a doctor, registered dietitian or qualified trainer. It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, for under-18s, or for eating disorders, diabetes on medication, kidney disease or other conditions: in those cases see a healthcare professional. If you feel pain or discomfort while training, stop and seek advice.

Nutrition information

Generic foods use average values from the USDA FoodData Central and BEDCA databases. Branded products come from Open Food Facts, a collaborative database under the ODbL licence; they may contain errors or be out of date. Always check the product label.
